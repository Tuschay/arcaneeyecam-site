Privacy Policy

Arcane Eye Cam for macOS · Effective 2 October 2026

Arcane Eye Cam does not collect, store or share any personal data. This policy explains what the app touches and why.

Camera and screen

With your permission, the app reads your Mac's camera and the display or window you choose to share. It combines them into one video on your Mac. That video goes only to the preview inside the app, to its output window, and to any app you select "Arcane Eye Cam" in as a camera. The developer never receives it.

Network

The app makes no network connections. It has no accounts, analytics, advertising or crash reporting of its own.

Settings stored on your Mac

Your layout, overlay and saved presets are kept in the app's local preferences on your Mac. Deleting the app removes them.

Third parties

Video call apps that you choose to send the camera to handle that video under their own privacy policies.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date. Questions: use the contact form. Messages sent through it are delivered by Formspree and used only to reply to you.
