Privacy Policy

Effective 2 October 2026

Arcane Eye Cam does not collect, store or share personal data. Here is what the app touches and why.

Camera and screen

With your permission, the app reads your Mac's camera and the display or window you choose. It combines them into one video on your Mac. That video goes only to the app's own preview and output window, and to any app where you pick "Arcane Eye Cam" as the camera. The developer never receives it.

Network

The app makes no network connections. It has no accounts, analytics, advertising or crash reporting of its own.

Settings on your Mac

Your layout, overlay and presets are saved in the app's preferences on your Mac. Deleting the app removes them.

Other apps

Call apps you send the camera to handle that video under their own privacy policies.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. Questions go through the contact form. Messages sent with it are delivered by Formspree and used only to reply to you.
