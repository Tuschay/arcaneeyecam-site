Arcane Eye Cam
Your screen, inside your camera

Show the slide and your face in the same frame. Arcane Eye Cam blends your camera and screen into one video, then any call app can use it like a webcam.

Get it on the Mac App Store Download for Windows

What it does

Four layouts

You with your screen beside you, your screen with you in the corner, or either one alone. One shortcut flips them.

Share one window

Pick a whole display or a single window, so your messages and other apps stay off the call.

Shape the overlay

Wide, square, circle or tall, in three sizes. Drag it where you want and it snaps to the edges.

A camera, not a share

Choose Arcane Eye Cam in the camera menu of Zoom, Teams, Meet or FaceTime. Nobody has to switch to a screen share.

Hotkeys and menu bar

Swap, hide or reshape the overlay without leaving the app you're presenting from.

Stays on your computer

No account, no tracking, no uploads. Video goes only to the call app you pick.

Arcane Eye Cam for Windows

Free, like the Mac version. The app runs on Windows 10 and 11. Casting only works on Windows 11. Windows 10 users can open the output window (Ctrl+Alt+W) and share that window in their call app instead.

Download the installer (65 MB)

The Windows version isn't sold through a store, so Windows and your browser will ask you to confirm a few times. That's expected. Here's what you'll see:

  1. Your browser may say the file "isn't commonly downloaded". Open the downloads list, choose the … menu, then Keep (and Keep anyway if asked).
  2. "Windows protected your PC" appears when you open the installer. Choose More info, then Run anyway.
  3. "Do you want to allow this app to make changes?" Choose Yes. The installer needs this once to add the camera for every app on your PC.

Then open Arcane Eye Cam, press Cast Arcane Eye, and choose "Arcane Eye Cam" as the camera in Zoom, Teams or Meet. Uninstall any time from Settings › Apps.
