Arcane Eye Cam

Put your screen in your camera. One video with you and your work, ready for Zoom, Microsoft Teams, Google Meet and any app that uses a Mac camera.

Download on the Mac App Store

Four layouts

Screen in camera, camera in screen, full camera or full screen. Swap with one shortcut.

Share just what you want

Pick a whole display or a single window so everything else stays private.

Shape the overlay

Wide, square, circle or tall, in three sizes. Drag it anywhere and it snaps to the edges.

Shows up as a camera

Choose Arcane Eye Cam as your camera in any call app. No separate screen share needed.

Menu bar and hotkeys

Control everything without leaving the app you are presenting.

Private by design

Everything is processed on your Mac. No accounts, no analytics, no uploads.
