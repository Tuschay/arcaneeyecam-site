Getting started

  1. Open Arcane Eye Cam and allow Camera access.
  2. Allow Screen Recording in System Settings > Privacy & Security, then quit and reopen the app. macOS only applies this permission after a restart of the app.
  3. Choose a layout and a display or window to share.
  4. Press Cast and allow the camera extension when macOS asks.
  5. In your call app, pick Arcane Eye Cam as the camera.

Common questions

I see my camera but not my screen. Grant Screen Recording, then quit and reopen the app.

Arcane Eye Cam doesn't appear in my call app. Make sure you pressed Cast and allowed the extension, then restart the call app so it rescans cameras.

A shortcut does nothing. Another app may already use that key combination. Close that app or change its shortcut.
